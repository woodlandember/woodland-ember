GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers before you submit an order.

Are the prices final?

Prices are estimates until the seller confirms product availability, package size and the final price.

How do I pay?

The website does not collect card details. The seller will contact you with available payment methods and instructions.

Is delivery included?

Delivery cost depends on your location and order size. It will be confirmed before you agree to the order.

Can I order in bulk?

Yes. Select Bulk & Pallet Order and explain your requirements in the additional details box.

Can I track my order online?

This website currently does not have an order-tracking page. The business will contact you directly.
